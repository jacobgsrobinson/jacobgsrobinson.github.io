Hermes Fleet Automations

A private, single-user automation system.

This application automates the day-to-day workflows of its owner: watching cloud folders for new files, keeping personal spreadsheets current, and publishing the owner's own video content. It connects to a single Google account, which belongs to the owner, and acts only on that owner's behalf and on the owner's instruction.

It is not a public product and has no other users.
